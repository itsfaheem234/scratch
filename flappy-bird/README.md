# flappy-bird
a scratch-based recreation of the classic flappy bird game, where the player controls a bird and tries to navigate through obstacles without crashing!

## about the game
flappy bird is a simple arcade-style game developed in scratch.

the player controls a bird that continuously moves through the environment. the goal is to keep the bird in the air and successfully pass through pipes to earn points.

```Current Status```: the game currently includes one pipe obstacle. additional pipes and obstacle generation are a work in progress.

## how to play
1. how to play
2. start the game.
3. press the spacebar to make the bird flap upward.
4. avoid hitting the pipe or falling off the screen.
5. try to pass through the gap between the pipes.
6. keep going and aim for a high score!
